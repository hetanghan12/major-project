# 📂 CloudSpace Navigation Guide

This document provides a detailed overview of the core navigation features shown in your sidebar.

---

### 📊 Dashboard
The Dashboard serves as the command center for your entire account, providing a snapshot of your storage health and recent activity. It features a modern donut chart that breaks down your storage usage by file types like Documents and Media. You can quickly see your total file counts, starred items, and real-time AI task balance. The recent activity list allows you to jump back into your most lately accessed files with a single click. It is designed to be the first and most useful page you see every time you log in.

### ⭐ Starred
The Starred section acts as your personal "Favorites" hub for the files you use most frequently. By clicking the star icon on any document or folder, you can bookmark it here for instant access without searching. This is perfect for important projects, frequently referenced spreadsheets, or essential study materials. Instead of digging through deep folders, you can rely on this curated list to keep your priority work at your fingertips. It saves you time and keeps your workflow organized according to what matters most to you.

### 👥 Shared With Me
The Shared With Me tab is where you access files and folders that other users have granted you permission to view or edit. It organizes incoming collaborative documents into a clean list, showing who the owner is and when the item was shared. This feature allows for seamless teamwork without needing to download and re-upload files between accounts. You can preview these files directly and interact with them based on the permissions (Viewer or Editor) set by the sharer. It’s the central place for all external contributions to your workspace.

### 📤 Shared By Me
The Shared By Me section gives you full visibility and management control over every file you have shared with others. Here, you can see exactly which of your documents are public or private and who currently has access to your data. It allows you to quickly revoke access or change permissions, such as turning an "Editor" back into a "Viewer." This is a critical security feature that ensures you always know who can see your files. It’s designed to give you peace of mind and complete transparency over your digital footprint.

### 🗑️ Trash
The Trash acts as a safety net for your documents, holding all deleted items before they are permanently removed. Files moved here do not immediately disappear, giving you the chance to restore them if you made a mistake. It prevents accidental data loss and allows you to "clean up" your workspace without the fear of losing important work forever. Items in the Trash can be individually restored to their original location or permanently deleted to free up storage space. It provides an essential layer of protection for your digital assets.
